# Jobs to Be Done (JTBD)

This document articulates the functional and emotional outcomes users seek when using the **Tip & Bill Splitter Calculator**.

---

## Job J-1 (Solo Quick Tipping)
When I finish a meal or receive a receipt, I want to quickly calculate standard or custom tips, so I can leave an appropriate tip without awkward mental arithmetic or delay.

---

## Job J-2 (Group Bill Payer)
When I pay the entire restaurant bill upfront, I want to calculate how much each person owes me including their portion of the tip, so I can collect the right amounts from everyone and ensure I'm not out of pocket.

---

## Job J-3 (Budget-Conscious Diner)
When I have a limited budget for dining out, I want to understand how different tip amounts affect what I will pay, so I can choose a tip percentage that shows appreciation without exceeding my budget.

---

## Job J-4 (Pre-Tax Service Tipping)
When a restaurant bill includes significant local and state sales tax, I want to calculate the tip on the pre-tax food and drink amount, so that I reward the service fairly without tipping on government taxes.

---

## Job J-5 (Cash & Transfer Rounding)
When our group pays in cash or settles up via peer-to-peer payment apps, I want to round the per-person amount or total bill up to the nearest whole dollar, so that we avoid dealing with loose change or fractional cents.
