# App Roles

This document defines the primary user roles and personas for the **Tip & Bill Splitter Calculator**.

---

## Role R-1: Solo Diner
A Solo Diner is an individual dining alone or picking up takeout who wants to calculate an appropriate tip quickly without doing mental arithmetic. They need to quickly input a bill amount, select a standard or custom tip percentage, and see the exact tip and total charge. They must never be delayed by complicated multi-step flows.

---

## Role R-2: Group Bill Payer / Organizer
A Group Bill Payer is a person dining with friends, colleagues, or family who pays the restaurant bill upfront or facilitates splitting the payment. They need to divide the bill and tip evenly among a group of people, understand per-person contributions, and copy a clear summary to paste into group chats (e.g., WhatsApp, SMS, Venmo). They must never be left out of pocket or calculate unequal shares due to rounding ambiguities.

---

## Role R-3: Budget-Conscious Diner
A Budget-Conscious Diner is a person with a limited budget who needs to understand exactly how much they will pay before deciding on a tip amount. They can adjust the tip percentage to see how it affects their individual share and the total. They must never be surprised by hidden fees or unclear calculations.

---

## Role R-4: Fair-Share Advocate (Pre-Tax Tipping)
A Fair-Share Advocate is a diner who believes tips should reward service staff based solely on the pre-tax food and beverage subtotal, rather than inflating the tip by applying it to government sales taxes. They need the ability to specify the included sales tax and have the tip calculated purely against the pre-tax amount.
